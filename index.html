Purchase Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Purchase Tracker is a personal, single-user Android app built by Yoav Brosh to track his own spending. It reads Google Wallet tap-to-pay notifications and, optionally, Gmail receipt emails, to keep a running tally of purchases on-device. This policy explains what data the app touches and what happens to it.

What data we access

  • Notifications: the app reads notifications posted by Google Wallet (com.google.android.apps.walletnfcrel) to extract a purchase amount and merchant name.
  • Gmail (optional): if you connect Gmail, the app requests read-only access (gmail.readonly) to search recent mail for receipt-looking messages and extract the amount and sender.

No other device data — contacts, location, files, or other apps — is accessed.

How your data is stored

All purchase data — amounts, merchants, categories, timestamps — is stored locally on your device in a local database. There is no backend server; nothing is uploaded anywhere by the app itself.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. The only network calls the app makes are directly to Google's Gmail API, using the access you grant, to read your own mail for the sole purpose of extracting purchase data onto your own device. No data is sent to the developer, an analytics service, or an ad network.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Gmail access anytime via the "Disconnect" button on the app's stats screen, or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Individual purchases can be deleted from the app's purchase list.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored purchase data.
